STM32F103C8T6 - TIM1_CH1 tren PA8

Timer va PWM, nhin nhu xung tren oscilloscope.

So tay truc quan de hieu `PSC`, `ARR`, `CCR1/Pulse`, duty cycle, LED dimming va dieu khien dong co DC qua L298N.

01 - Timer

Timer la bo dem thoi gian.

Trong project nay, timer dem moi `1 us`. Khi dem tu `0` den `99`, no tao ra mot vong dem dai `100 us`.

Buoc 1

Chia clock bang PSC

f_CNT = 72 MHz / (71 + 1) = 1 MHz

Counter tang 1,000,000 lan moi giay.

Buoc 2

Tinh thoi gian 1 tick

T_tick = 1 / 1 MHz = 1 us

Moi lan counter tang len 1 thi mat 1 micro giay.

Buoc 3

ARR tao chu ky

ARR = 99 -> 0..99 = 100 tick

Dem tu 0 den 99 la 100 gia tri vi dem bao gom ca 0.

0123...9899reset ve 0

02 - PWM

PWM la bat/tat nhanh trong tung chu ky.

`ARR` tao khung thoi gian cua chu ky. `CCR1/Pulse` quyet dinh phan HIGH nam trong khung do.

T_PWM (ARR + 1) x T_tick = 100 x 1 us = 100 us
f_PWM 1 / 100 us = 10,000 Hz = 10 kHz

03 - LED

LED nhin thay do sang trung binh.

Chan PWM van chi HIGH hoac LOW. Nhung vi `10 kHz` qua nhanh voi mat nguoi, LED trong nhu dang co nhieu muc sang.

Duty thap

`CCR1 = 25`: LED duoc cap dien 25% thoi gian, nen nhin mo.

Duty cao

`CCR1 = 75`: LED duoc cap dien 75% thoi gian, nen nhin sang hon.

04 - Motor DC

Motor cam nhan nang luong trung binh.

`PA8 PWM -> L298N ENA`. Khi PA8 HIGH, ENA bat va motor duoc cap nang luong. Khi PA8 LOW, ENA tat trong khoang rat ngan.

Ket noi dieu khien

  • PA8 -> TIM1_CH1 PWM -> L298N ENA
  • PB12 -> IN1
  • PB13 -> IN2
  • 12 V ngoai -> motor supply cua L298N

Huong quay

Luu y an toan: Khong cap nguon motor tu Blue Pill. Motor dung nguon ngoai 12 V, va STM32 GND phai noi chung voi L298N GND.

Can nho

Nhung cho de nham.

`ARR = 99` khong phai 10,000 count

`ARR = 99` nghia la dem `0..99`, tong cong 100 tick trong mot chu ky PWM.

`10 kHz` la so chu ky moi giay

Vi mot chu ky dai `100 us`, nen moi giay co 10,000 chu ky PWM.

`CCR1` khong doi tan so PWM

`CCR1` chi doi thoi gian HIGH, tuc duty cycle. `ARR` moi la thu dinh do dai chu ky.
